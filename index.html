About REI

More Than Real Estate.
A Platform for Real Estate Innovation.

The real estate industry needs more than new buildings. It needs better ways to design, finance, build, transact, manage and invest in property.

The Real Estate Incubator exists to bridge this gap by bringing together entrepreneurial talent, industry expertise, innovative ideas and capital.

We work with founders, property entrepreneurs, developers and industry professionals to turn promising ideas and opportunities into successful ventures.

01
Beyond PropTech.
Technology is one route to innovation — not the only one. We back new models wherever they create value.
02
Beyond Traditional Property Development.
We structure opportunities around commercial logic, partnership and execution, not square metres alone.
03
Beyond Conventional Investment.
We combine venture building with selective capital and strategic financing partnerships.

Areas of Focus

Where Innovation Meets Opportunity.

Six domains where we actively look for ideas, ventures and opportunities across the real estate and built-environment ecosystem.

01

Property & Construction Technology

Digital platforms, tools and systems improving how property is built, managed and transacted.

02

Housing & Development Innovation

New approaches to affordable housing, delivery models and development economics.

03

Real Estate Finance Innovation

Mortgage, rental, fractional, structured and alternative financing solutions for property.

04

Innovative Property Ventures

Unconventional deals, joint ventures, asset repositioning and new commercial structures.

05

Built Environment & Sustainability

Materials, energy, climate-resilient design and sustainable construction practice.

06

Emerging Real Estate Business Models

Service, operating and platform models redefining how property creates value.

Innovation does not have to be technological. A better way to structure a transaction, finance a development, deliver housing or operate an asset is just as relevant to us as a software product.

How It Works

From Idea to Investable Venture.

A disciplined, six-stage pathway that takes promising ideas and opportunities from first conversation to commercial execution.

01

Discover

We identify promising founders, ideas and property opportunities.

02

Evaluate

We assess commercial potential, feasibility and execution readiness.

03

Incubate

We provide expertise, resources, partnerships and hands-on development.

04

Structure

We develop commercially viable business and investment propositions.

05

Fund

We deploy selective capital and connect opportunities with financing partners.

06

Scale

We support commercialisation, execution and long-term growth.
